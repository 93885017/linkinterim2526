Privacy Policy Statement
General Statement of Policy
  1. This Statement is made by Link Asset Management Limited (“Link Asset Management”) as manager of Link Real Estate Investment Trust (“Link REIT”) and Link Properties Limited (collectively “we”, “our” or “us”), in accordance with the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) of the Laws of Hong Kong Special Administrative Region ('the PDPO'). We are committed to ensuring that your personal data are handled in accordance with the provisions of the PDPO. We collect and use your personal data to provide you with better customer services and products.
  2. We collect adequate, but not excessive, personal data by lawful and fair means only for lawful purposes related to our business and activities.
  3. We take all reasonably practicable steps to ensure that personal data collected or retained are accurate, having regard to the purposes for which they are to be used.
  4. We take all reasonably practicable steps to ensure that the personal data are not kept longer than is necessary for the purposes for which they are to be used.
  5. We use the personal data collected only for the purposes or directly related purposes for which the data were to be used at the time of collection, unless the individual concerned has given express consent for a change of use or such use is permitted by law.
  6. We take all reasonably practicable steps to ensure that personal data are protected against unauthorised or accidental access, processing, erasure or other use.
  7. We take all reasonably practicable steps to ensure that a person can be informed of the kinds of personal data that we hold and the purposes for which the data are to be used.
  8. We permit persons to request access to and/or correction of personal data held by us which they are the data subjects. We process any such access/correction requests in a manner permitted or required by law.
  9. All our staff are required to observe our security and confidentiality obligations.
Collection - Why We Collect Your Data
  10. From time to time, we may collect your personal data to provide our services to you and in an effort to improve your customer experience. You are not obliged to provide your personal data to us but if you do not, we may not be able to provide you with some of the information, materials, services and/or products of Link Asset Management and/or Link Properties Limited.
  11. We will indicate in the relevant forms or applications if the required personal data are for obligatory or voluntary purposes. If personal data are to be used for an obligatory purpose, you MUST provide your personal data to us if you want us to provide the service for which you are applying. If personal data are only to be used for a voluntary purpose, you can tell us not to use your personal data for that purpose and we will refrain from doing so.
  12. If you are under the age of 13, you must ask your parent or guardian before providing any personal data to us.
Data Security
  13. We strive at all times to ensure that your personal data will be protected against unauthorised or accidental access, processing, erasure or other use. We maintain this commitment to data security by implementing appropriate physical, electronic and managerial measures to safeguard and secure your personal data.
Control of Personal Data
  14. Link Asset Management's Corporate Data Privacy Officer or a designated person is responsible for assessing, monitoring and reviewing personal data protection measures to ensure compliance with the requirements of the PDPO.
Charges
  15. In accordance with the PDPO, we may charge data subjects reasonable charges incurred in relation to administering and complying with their data access or correction requests.
